Meet Our CEO

Led by Karla Gomez

Koinonia Fun Childcare With Titi Bili, LLC is led by Karla Gomez, a childcare provider with the licenses, training, experience, and competencies required by the State of Connecticut.

Our mission is to provide a warm, safe, and family-centered environment where every child feels loved, supported, and encouraged to grow.
